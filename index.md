We're San Mateo County's chapter of [YIMBY Action](https://yimbyaction.org/) — local activists advocating for abundant housing at all income levels, stronger tenant protections, and better transit across the Peninsula.

This site hosts open-source data tools built by P4E volunteers. For campaigns, endorsements, and how to get involved, visit **[peninsulaforeveryone.org](https://www.peninsulaforeveryone.org)**.

## Projects

### [Peninsula Permit Tracker →](https://peninsulaforeveryone.github.io/permit-timeline-tracker/)

How long does your city take to approve housing? Peninsula jurisdictions ranked by a friction score combining RHNA progress, application-to-permit conversion rates, median approval timelines, and ADU statutory compliance under AB 68/881. Built on HCD Annual Progress Report data.

[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/PeninsulaForEveryone/permit-timeline-tracker)

### [Enrollment vs. Housing →](https://peninsulaforeveryone.github.io/peninsula-enrollment-decline/)

K-12 enrollment in most San Mateo County districts peaked around 2015–2017 and has fallen since — including in the districts building the most units. A direct rebuttal to "new housing will overwhelm our schools."

[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/PeninsulaForEveryone/peninsula-enrollment-decline)

### [SB 79 Analysis →](https://peninsulaforeveryone.github.io/sb79-analysis/public/)

Interactive map showing which parcels California SB 79 would upzone near high-quality transit, with potential housing capacity calculated by transit-proximity tier (200ft, ¼-mile, ½-mile). Currently scoped to Berkeley as a methodology proof.

[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/PeninsulaForEveryone/sb79-analysis)
